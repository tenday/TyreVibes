# Product Guidelines - TyreVibes

## Visual Identity
- **High-Tech & Precision:** Dark mode by default with neon blue/green accents. The UI should feel futuristic and precise, mirroring the advanced LiDAR and AI technologies powering the app.

## Communication Style
- **Concise & Utility-focused:** Text should be minimal and direct. Prioritize speed and clarity, giving the user exactly what they need to know for vehicle maintenance without unnecessary filler.

## UI/UX Principles
- **One-Handed Optimization:** Layouts are designed for easy interaction while the user is standing near or inspecting a vehicle.
- **Visual Data First:** Leverage charts and 3D tire models to communicate wear levels and status at a glance.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Provide tactile haptic feedback and clear visual cues during intensive tasks like LiDAR scanning or AI inference.
- **High-Visibility Design:** High contrast and large targets ensure the app is usable in bright sunlight or dark garage environments.

## Notification Strategy
- **Smart Management:** Use a multi-layered approach to notifications:
  - **Grouped Insights:** Proactively group non-critical tasks to avoid clutter.
  - **Urgency Priority:** Reserve immediate alerts for critical safety issues like excessive tire wear.
  - **User Control:** Allow users to customize reminder lead times for administrative deadlines (insurance, tax).
